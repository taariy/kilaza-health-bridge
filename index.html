Kilaza Health Bridge

A private, single-user personal utility.

Kilaza Health Bridge reads the owner's own daily step total from the Google Health API (dailyRollUp, all sources) and records it in the owner's private Kilaza health ledger. It is used by one person, on one personal computer, for personal fitness tracking.

The app requests a single read-only scope: googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly. It never writes to Google Health and never shares data with anyone.
