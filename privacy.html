Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026.

Who this app is for

Kilaza Health Bridge is a personal, single-user utility. The only user is its owner and developer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

With the read-only scope googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly, the app reads one value: the owner's own deduplicated daily step total for the current day from the Google Health API. It reads no other health data and writes nothing back to Google.

How the data is used and stored

The step total is written into the owner's own private Kilaza repository, which he alone controls, so that his personal dashboard can show daily activity. OAuth credentials stay on the owner's own computer, outside any repository. Nothing is sent to any third party, sold, shared, or used for advertising, profiling, or model training.

Retention and deletion

Step totals stay in the owner's private ledger until he deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all further reads.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
